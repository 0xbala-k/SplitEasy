## SplitEasy Privacy Policy
  Last updated: May 2026

  ### 1. Information We Collect
  SplitEasy connects to your bank account via Plaid to retrieve transaction data (merchant name, amount,
  date). This data is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to SplitEasy servers.

  ### 2. How We Use Your Information
  Transaction data is used solely to display pending expenses and facilitate bill splitting with friends
  via Splitwise. We do not sell, share, or transmit your financial data to any third party other than
  Splitwise (when you explicitly submit a split).

  ### 3. Third-Party Services
  - Plaid: Used to access bank transaction data. Plaid's privacy policy applies to all bank
  authentication. See plaid.com/legal.
  - Splitwise: Expense data is shared with Splitwise only upon your explicit action. See Splitwise's
  privacy policy.

  ### 4. Data Storage
  Transaction data is stored in an encrypted database on your device. Plaid access tokens are stored in
  the iOS Keychain and never leave your device.

  ### 5. Data Deletion
  You can delete all locally stored data at any time by tapping "Disconnect bank" in Settings, or by
  uninstalling the app.
