How it works

Three steps to split anything

No manual entry. No chasing friends. SplitEasy does the heavy lifting.

1
🏦
Connect your bank

Link your bank account securely via Plaid with read-only access. We never see or store your login credentials.

2
🧾
Review transactions

SplitEasy automatically imports new transactions and surfaces the ones worth splitting — restaurants, travel, groceries, and more.

3
✅
Split with friends

Pick a friend from your Splitwise contacts, choose equal or custom split amounts, and create the expense instantly.

4
📊
Track history

Every split is recorded locally and synced to Splitwise. Review what you've split and what's still pending — all in one place.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Built for people who share expenses regularly and want a zero-friction way to keep things fair.

💳
Multi-bank support

Connect multiple bank accounts or cards. SplitEasy consolidates transactions from all sources into a single feed.

⚖️
Equal & custom splits

Split evenly with one tap, or dial in exact amounts per person for those dinners where everyone ordered differently.

🔄
Real-time sync

Pull the latest transactions on demand. Plaid's incremental sync keeps things fast without re-importing what you've already seen.

📱
Splitwise native

Expenses are created directly in Splitwise via the official API. Your friends get notified the moment you hit Submit.

📋
Local history

A full log of split and skipped transactions lives on-device in SQLite — no cloud account required for your personal history.

🛡️
Privacy first

Bank tokens are stored in the iOS Secure Enclave. Transaction data stays on your device. We don't sell your data to anyone.

Security

Built with security as a foundation

Every design decision in SplitEasy was made to protect your financial data.

🔐
Secure Enclave storage

Plaid access tokens are stored in iOS Secure Enclave (expo-secure-store). They never leave your device unencrypted.

👁️
Read-only access

SplitEasy requests read-only bank access via Plaid. We can view transactions — we cannot initiate transfers or payments.

🚫
No credential storage

Your bank username and password pass directly through Plaid's encrypted SDK and are never transmitted to our servers.

🔒
TLS 1.2+ in transit

All API communication between the app and our Cloudflare Worker backend uses TLS 1.2 or higher with certificate validation.

🗑️
Right to deletion

Disconnect your bank at any time to permanently delete all local transactions and tokens. No data lingers on our infrastructure.

📜
Transparent policies

Our Privacy Policy and data retention rules are published and written in plain language — no legalese.
